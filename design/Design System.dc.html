Oigo Design System
v1.0 · macOS app + marketing · Aug 2026
Oigo dresses as a native macOS citizen: system fonts, system colors, system materials. The design system adds only what the platform doesn't provide — the mark, state semantics, HUD anatomy, voice. When in doubt, do what macOS would do.

10

Voice & copy

Oigo speaks like a careful engineer: precise about what happened, honest about what it can't verify, never alarmed. It says “Paste attempted,” not “Pasted” — it can't know. Failures lead with what was preserved.
Principles
1.Claim only what you know. “Paste attempted”, “Copied to Clipboard” — never assert success inside another app.
2.Lead with preservation. Every failure message starts from what survived: “Recording preserved.”
3.Name the fix. Every notice pairs a problem with exactly one action button.
4.Sentence case, no exclamation marks, no blame. “Microphone unavailable”, not “Oops! Mic error!”
