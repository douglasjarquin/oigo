Privacy

Your voice never leaves this Mac.

Recording, transcription, and cleanup all run on Apple's on-device speech models. Oigo has no server, no account, no analytics — and the source is public, so you don't have to take our word for it.

History & recovery

A dictation is never lost.

Audio is written to disk from the first word, before transcription starts. If a paste fails, a mic disconnects, or your Mac sleeps mid-sentence, the recording survives — open History, retry transcription, copy the text again. Audio follows your retention setting; transcripts stay until you delete them.
